Original IP from AruaAxys

Sight

"There has to be a better way to live than just trying to live."

Sight is an original anime about exhaustion, survival mode, and the invisible weight people carry, and the search for a truer way of living beyond just making it through.

It starts small and grows in the open:

Character introductions Lore drops Manga and webtoon panels Motion-comic scenes Micro-episodes Teaser trailers

For creators

Share your anime. Keep your rights.

A core goal of AniSoul is a legal lane for Black anime creators and other approved creators to share their anime, shorts, pilots, trailers and series while keeping ownership of their work, with permissions and creator protections built in.
